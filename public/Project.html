Malin + Goetz — a refined retail experience

A precise storefront and interior build for the apothecary brand's Silverlake location — finish-forward, on schedule, ready for opening day.

The Project

Retail that reads as considered, not loud

In retail, every detail matters — design, functionality, and ambiance shape the customer experience and, ultimately, the bottom line. For Malin + Goetz, econstruct delivered a storefront and interior tuned to the brand's restrained, material-honest aesthetic.

We coordinated millwork, lighting, MEP and finish trades against a fixed launch date, managing long-lead fixtures so the schedule never slipped. The result is a space that feels effortless — which is the hardest kind to build.

  • Custom millwork & display fixtures
  • Storefront & signage coordination
  • Full MEP & specialty lighting
  • Delivered ahead of launch
