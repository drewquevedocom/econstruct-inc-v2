How We Work

Our tailored end-to-end construction process

Every project is unique. Our flexible process adapts to your needs — renovating existing structures or building ground-up.

01

Initial Concept & Budgeting

We start with your vision, scope and a realistic budget — so the numbers are honest before a line is drawn.

02

Design, Engineering & Plan Development

We partner with your architect and engineers to develop plans that build cleanly and protect the budget.

03

Permitting & Concrete / Framing Construction

We navigate LADBS and Health Department plan check, then break ground on concrete and framing.

Trade coordination through the finish stage — millwork, MEP, fixtures and the details that define the space.
