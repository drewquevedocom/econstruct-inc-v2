Every agency that touches a commercial permit in Los Angeles runs its own review on its own schedule, and the single biggest scheduling error is treating them as one combined timeline instead of several parallel ones. This is a 2026 reference for how each agency typically factors into a commercial project schedule.

## Building Plan Check (LADBS)

Standard commercial tenant improvement plan check with the Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety typically runs several weeks to a few months, depending on drawing completeness and current jurisdiction backlog. Ground-up construction and projects with structural or occupancy changes generally take longer and may require multiple review cycles.

## Fire Department Review

Fire prevention review runs separately from building plan check and covers life safety systems, hood and suppression systems, and fire access requirements. Submitting coordinated drawings to both building and fire review at the same time, rather than sequentially, is the most effective way to keep this from adding time on top of building plan check.

## Health Department Review (Food Facilities)

Food service and food production facilities need a separate health department review of layout, drainage, and equipment clearances, which runs alongside — not instead of — building and fire review. Facilities that skip early health department coordination often face late-stage layout corrections that reopen an otherwise finished plan check.

## Utility Coordination Timelines

Electrical, water, and gas service upgrades are scheduled by the utility provider, not the city, and do not accelerate because a building permit has been issued. These requests should be submitted as early as load calculations allow, tracked as a parallel timeline alongside permitting rather than a step that starts after permits are in hand.

## Entitlement and CEQA Review

Projects requiring discretionary approval or environmental review should budget significant additional time before plan check even begins, since entitlement and CEQA review are front-loaded processes that happen ahead of, not during, standard permit review.
