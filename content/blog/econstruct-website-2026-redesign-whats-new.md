After more than a year of building a website that quietly undersold what econstruct actually does, we rebuilt it — properly. The new site went live in June 2026, and this post covers what changed, why, and what it means for how clients find and evaluate us.

## Why We Rebuilt It

The old site led with luxury residential construction — custom homes, fire rebuilds, and modernizations. That work is real and ongoing, and we're proud of it. But it represented roughly half of what econstruct actually builds.

The other half — restaurants, retail stores, office tenant improvements, multi-location commercial build-outs, and food distribution facilities — was either buried, mislabeled, or missing entirely. The contact page had the wrong phone number. The About page described us as a "premier residential contractor." The Projects section showed only a handful of our portfolio.

That didn't reflect 634 completed projects. It didn't reflect 51 years of combined experience. And it didn't reflect the commercial construction discipline that is, genuinely, what sets econstruct apart in the Los Angeles market.

So we fixed it.

---

## What Changed on the Homepage

The homepage was rebuilt in an Indutri-style layout — the same structure that reflected the original econstruct Inc. brand — with a full-bleed cinematic video hero and a commercial-forward section structure.

**New sections include:**

- **Video hero** — econstruct's real footage, compressed for fast loading, with the headline *"Constructing Exceptional Spaces to Live, Work, Shop & Eat"*
- **About section** — the real story: 51 years of experience, Frank Neimroozi's commercial construction roots, single-team accountability
- **6 service cards** — Restaurant & Bar, Retail Fit-Out, Office/TI, Food Distribution & Cold Storage, Custom Homes & ADUs, Architecture & Expediting
- **Stats counter** — 634 projects, 51 years, CA Lic #964015, animated on scroll
- **Awards section** — Best Restaurant Contractor, Santa Monica 2024 and Glendale 2022
- **Project carousel** — Hutchinson Cocktails & Grill, Hal's Bar, El Pollo Loco, Rothy's, Jersey Mike's, Odd One Out, and more
- **Client testimonials** — John McKeon (Rothy's), Rick B. (Jersey Mike's), Ali H. (Prosper Architects), and others
- **Client logo marquee** — El Pollo Loco, 800 Degrees, Groundwork Coffee, 85°C Bakery, Rothy's, Subway, Hal's Bar
- **Food & Distribution spotlight** — the new division featured on the homepage with all six sub-pages

## Projects Page — Fixed and Filterable

The projects page was one of the most broken parts of the old site. It showed a "19+" stat that didn't reflect our actual portfolio, used the wrong images for several projects, and hid most projects below a single featured card.

**What's fixed:**

- All projects now display in a filterable grid (Restaurant & Bar / Retail / Commercial tabs) — every project visible at once
- Project images corrected — each project shows its own build, not a placeholder
- Stat corrected to 634+ completed projects
- Featured badge on the portfolio's strongest showcase projects
