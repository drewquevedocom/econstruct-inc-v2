## How to Vet Any Contractor Before You Hire

Before signing with any residential contractor in Los Angeles — regardless of how they appeared on a list or how impressive their website looks — run through these verification steps:

- **Confirm an active CSLB license** at the California Contractors State License Board's online lookup tool. A valid license means the contractor has passed trade exams, carries required insurance, and is bonded.
- **Know the legal down payment cap.** California law caps the down payment a contractor can legally request at 10% of the project cost or $1,000 — whichever is less. Any contractor demanding more is violating state law.
- **Get insurance certificates in writing.** Ask for the general liability and workers' compensation certificates directly — not just their word they have coverage — and verify the policies are current.
- **Call at least three references** from projects completed in the past two years at a similar scope and budget. The questions that matter most: Did the project finish on schedule? Did the final cost match the estimate? How did the contractor handle unexpected conditions?

> **Key Takeaway:** License verification at cslb.ca.gov takes two minutes and is the single most important step before any other evaluation.
