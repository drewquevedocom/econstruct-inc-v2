What It Is

One team accountable for the whole build

As your general contractor, econstruct owns the build end to end. We hold the schedule, manage every subcontractor, control the budget, and answer for quality — so you have a single point of accountability from the first concept meeting to the day you get the keys.

Our approach emphasizes robust project management and decades of experience with complex, fast-moving jobs. We pay meticulous attention to detail and adhere rigorously to timelines — especially when managing materials with long lead times — to keep your project moving and your opening date intact.

What's included

Preconstruction & budgeting

Scope, value engineering and a realistic budget before design is locked — so the numbers hold.

Scheduling & phasing

A build sequence that manages long-lead items and protects your launch or move-in date.

Trade coordination

Vetted subcontractors managed daily in the field, with one team answerable for the result.
