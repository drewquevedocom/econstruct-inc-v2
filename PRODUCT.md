# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary users are high-intent property owners, developers, brand operators, and business decision-makers evaluating a premium general contractor in Los Angeles. This includes luxury residential homeowners, restaurant founders, retail operators, office tenants, and commercial project stakeholders who need confidence before requesting a consultation. They arrive from branded search, local service queries, referrals, and project-specific research, and they need proof that econstruct can handle complex, design-sensitive work with precision.

## Product Purpose

This website exists to convert qualified Los Angeles construction demand into consultations by presenting econstruct as the most credible choice for high-end residential and commercial construction. Success means preserving existing organic visibility from the current WordPress site, improving lead quality, strengthening local authority in affluent Los Angeles neighborhoods, and making it easy for visitors to understand services, review project proof, and take the next step.

## Brand Personality

Exacting, confident, elevated.

The voice should feel premium and direct without sounding inflated. It should communicate operational rigor, design fluency, and practical expertise. The emotional outcome is trust: visitors should feel that econstruct is disciplined enough for complex builds, polished enough for luxury clients, and experienced enough to reduce risk.

## Anti-references

This should not look like a generic construction template, a stock-heavy builder directory, or a loud agency landing page. Avoid gimmicky luxury cues, fake scarcity, black-and-gold cliches, overused SaaS card grids, and thin geo pages that exist only to stuff keywords. It should not feel rustic, blue-collar, bargain-focused, or amateur.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with proof, not claims.
2. Make premium feel operational, not decorative.
3. Preserve search equity while upgrading perception.
4. Keep paths to inquiry obvious on every key page.
5. Use local authority where it helps decisions, not as filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for core marketing flows. Respect reduced-motion preferences, preserve strong text contrast, and keep navigation, forms, and calls to action usable on mobile and desktop. Content should stay readable for users scanning quickly under high cognitive load, especially on long service and blog pages.
